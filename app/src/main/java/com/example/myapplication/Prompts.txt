1. The "Golden Path" (Routine Regularity Score)
This measures how closely your current week adheres to your historical baseline. It answers the question: "Is this a normal week, or is my schedule chaotic right now?"

The Concept: The app builds a "fingerprint" of a typical Monday, Tuesday, etc., based on historical data. It then compares the current day's timeline to that fingerprint.

The Visuals:

A simple percentage (e.g., "82% Routine Adherence").

A comparative timeline: Your historical average timeline for a Tuesday faded in the background, overlaid with your actual timeline for this Tuesday.

The Insight: It visually highlights anomalies. For instance, if you usually study at the library from 2 PM to 5 PM, but this week you were at home, that block will be flagged as a deviation.
